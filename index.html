THE WATER CHAIN · EASY

1 · Slide to merge

Swipe or use the arrow keys to slide every tile. Two matching tiles merge into the next tier.

2 · Build a Well

Merge two Tanks into a Well. It leaves the board and serves a community.

3 · Clear contaminants

Contaminants can't merge, cost 20 points when they appear and spread to empty neighboring tiles over time. Merge right next to one to wash it away.

DIFFICULTY

  • Easy: 6-tier chain that ends at the Well. Contaminants spread every 20 moves.
  • Normal: 7-tier chain with the Water Tower on top. Contaminants spread every 10 moves.
  • Hard: 7-tier chain with the Water Tower on top. Contaminants spread every 6 moves.

The game ends when the board fills up. Contaminants show up more often the longer you play.
