THE WATER CHAIN

1 · Move to merge

Swipe or use the arrow keys to slide all tiles. Two matching tiles combine into the next tier.

2 · Build wells

Merge two Tanks into a Well. It leaves the board and serves a community.

3 · Clear contaminants

Contaminants can't merge and cost you points when they appear. Make a match right next to one to wash it away.

Game ends when the board fills up. Contaminants show up more often the longer you play.
